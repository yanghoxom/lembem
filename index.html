01 / About LEMBEM

Our Mission & Public Benefit Mandate

Founded on 15 May 2025 as a registered non-profit scientific and technological organization in Vietnam, LEMBEM bridges theoretical computation and real-world community challenges. Our mission is to develop and distribute free, open-access scientific models, datasets and technological tools for researchers, universities, farmers and underserved communities — without commercial barriers.

We prioritize transparent methodology, reproducibility, practical engineering and accessible knowledge dissemination.

100%

Open-Access Commitment

Research outputs are intended for public access, with CC BY-NC 4.0 for VietBioNet data and appropriate open-source licensing for software once released.

0%

Commercial Access Fees

Our public-interest research model is designed around grants, donations and mission-aligned sponsorships rather than paywalled scientific access.

Non-Distribution Commitment

LEMBEM's stated operating mandate is scientific, educational and public-benefit research. Under its non-distribution principle, surplus funds, grants and donations are directed toward research infrastructure, open-source community projects and scientific education — not private shareholder dividends.
