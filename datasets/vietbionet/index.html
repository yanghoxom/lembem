Research scope & public benefit

VietBioNet brings together flora and agricultural bio-marker research data to support environmental analysis, university teaching and independent bio-informatics investigations. It forms part of LEMBEM's public-interest commitment to sharing scientific knowledge without proprietary access barriers.

Researchers should consult the accompanying dataset documentation for provenance, coverage, limitations and correct citation details before using the records in analyses or publications.
