Privacy Policy

This notice explains how information is handled when you visit LEMBEM LAB or contact the institute at Chi Lang Commune, Bac Ninh Province, Vietnam.

Information you choose to share

The VietBioNet page provides a dataset access request form. If you submit it, we collect your name, email, optional institution, research purpose and consent indication to evaluate the request and reply. We also process correspondence sent directly to our email. We do not provide account registration or on-site payment forms.

Third-party resources

The VietBioNet request form transmits submitted details to FormSubmit (formsubmit.co), a third-party form-delivery provider, for forwarding to contact@lab.lembem.com. FormSubmit reports that it retains submissions for up to 30 days. The website also uses Google Fonts, Tailwind's hosted script and a hosted background photograph; their servers may receive IP addresses and browser request details. External research links are governed by their own privacy policies.

Why we use contact details

We use email messages and VietBioNet form submissions to evaluate dataset access requests and respond to research, collaboration and grant enquiries. We do not intentionally sell personal information. A successful form acknowledgement indicates the request service accepted the submission; it does not mean dataset access has been approved.

Your choices & contact

To request access, correction or deletion of information you have sent to us, contact contact@lab.lembem.com. Requests are subject to applicable legal and recordkeeping obligations.
