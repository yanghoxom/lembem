Privacy Policy

This notice explains how information is handled when you visit LEMBEM LAB or contact the institute at Chi Lang Commune, Bac Ninh Province, Vietnam.

Information you choose to share

The website does not operate an on-site registration or donation form. If you contact us by email, your email provider and the recipients process the information you decide to send, such as your name, email address, institution and message.

Third-party resources

This static website uses Google Fonts, Tailwind's hosted script and a hosted background photograph. Depending on browser configuration, those hosts may receive technical request metadata, including IP address and browser information. Following links to externally hosted project repositories or AgriSense subjects you to those sites' policies.

Why we use contact details

We use correspondence to respond to collaboration enquiries, requests for scientific materials, technical discussion and grant-related communications. We do not intentionally sell personal information.

Your choices & contact

To request access, correction or deletion of information you have sent to us, contact contact@lembem.com. Requests are subject to applicable legal and recordkeeping obligations.
