Governance & Transparency

An overview of the LEMBEM research initiative's public-benefit commitments, governance intentions and institutional disclosure status.

Identity & institutional status

Public descriptions of mission or organizational category must not be treated as evidence of legal incorporation, tax-exempt status or governmental recognition. The full legal charter and registration particulars will be published after verification.

Non-distribution principle

The initiative is committed to pursuing scientific, educational and public-benefit outcomes. The intended non-distribution framework directs net surplus, grants and donated resources to public-interest research, open-source infrastructure and educational initiatives, rather than private shareholder dividends.

Any legally enforceable non-distribution obligation is subject to the organization's finalized governing documents and applicable local law.

Leadership and oversight

AgriSense publicly identifies Hung Dao as its founder and engineering lead, and Phung Ha as an agricultural research contributor. These affiliations do not, by themselves, establish statutory LEMBEM board appointments.

Formal board membership, research-council appointments and any authorized legal representative will be disclosed after confirmation.

Open access and intellectual property

LEMBEM aims to publish appropriate software, methods and research resources openly. Individual repositories, datasets and papers carry their own stated licence. Planned licence categories include MIT for selected software and Creative Commons Attribution for eligible data and scholarly publications.

Nothing on this page guarantees public release of every research dataset, particularly materials involving confidential, private or third-party information.
