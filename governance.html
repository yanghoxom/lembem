Non-Distribution & Public-Benefit Commitment

LEMBEM operates for scientific, educational and public-benefit purposes. No part of the organization's net earnings or grants is intended for distribution as private shareholder dividends. Grants, donations and operational surpluses are reinvested in scientific infrastructure, open research, education and community technology projects.

Open-Access Research & Licensing

LEMBEM advances openly accessible scientific models, educational media and ecological data. VietBioNet is described as an open-access dataset under Creative Commons Attribution-NonCommercial 4.0. EcoModel-VN is currently in private development; a software licence will be declared if the project is publicly released.

Specific software and dataset licences govern the corresponding releases. Original website content is licensed under CC BY-NC 4.0 unless otherwise indicated; third-party materials remain subject to their respective rights.
